Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what information Ilactic collects through this website and its features — the Join Community popup, the AI audit form, and the contact form — and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • Join Community: your name and WhatsApp number, submitted when you join our WhatsApp group through the popup.
  • AI audit request: your website URL and email address, so we can run and send back an audit.
  • Contact form: whatever name, email, and message you choose to send us.
  • Nothing else, automatically: we don't run third-party analytics or ad-tracking scripts on this site. The only browser storage we use is a small session-only flag that remembers you've dismissed a popup, which clears itself and never leaves your browser.

3. How we use it

We use what you submit only to do the thing you asked for: add you to the WhatsApp community and send its daily digest, run and deliver your AI audit, or reply to your message. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for unrelated marketing.

4. Where it's stored

Join Community submissions are stored in a Google Sheet that Ilactic owns and controls, via Google's infrastructure. We may use similar lightweight, reputable third-party tools (such as a form-handling or spreadsheet service) for the other forms as they go live. We do not use these services to sell to third parties beyond operating the service itself.

5. WhatsApp

Joining our community happens through WhatsApp's own group-invite system — WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms govern that part of the experience once you join. We only see the name and number you typed into our popup, not your other WhatsApp activity.

6. How long we keep it

We keep submitted information for as long as it's useful for the purpose you gave it to us (e.g. for as long as you're in the community, or until your audit/contact request is resolved), and delete or anonymize it after that unless you ask us to remove it sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

To do any of this, email hello@ilactic.com with your name and phone/email so we can find your record.

8. Children

This site and its community aren't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our features change. Material changes will update the "last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@ilactic.com.
